# Human Resources

The language used by this HR app for employee management and leave approval.

## Language

**Employee**:
A person employed by the organization whose employment information is managed in the HR app.
_Avoid_: Staff record, worker record

**Leave request**:
An employee's request for time away from work that requires approval.
_Avoid_: Holiday request, vacation request

**Department**:
A group within the organization to which employees belong.
_Avoid_: Company, organization

**Employee directory**:
A browsable listing of employees, their departments, and contact details.
_Avoid_: Team director, staff database

**Manager**:
An employee responsible for approving leave requests from their direct reports.
_Avoid_: Approver role, team director

**HR Administrator**:
A user responsible for managing employee records and overseeing leave across the organization.
_Avoid_: Superuser, manager

**Shared calendar**:
A calendar of approved employee leave and public holidays for the organization.
_Avoid_: Approval queue, pending leave calendar

**Annual leave**:
Time away from work requested against an employee's annual leave entitlement.
_Avoid_: Vacation balance, holiday allowance

**Sick leave**:
Time away from work requested because of illness.
_Avoid_: Annual leave, vacation

**Public holiday**:
A date maintained by HR as a non-working day for the company.
_Avoid_: Employee leave, weekend

**Leave entitlement**:
The number of days of a leave type allocated to an employee for a calendar year.
_Avoid_: Remaining balance, accrual

**Available leave balance**:
An employee's entitlement minus approved leave and days reserved by pending requests for that year and leave type.
_Avoid_: Entitlement, total allowance

**Leave history**:
A record of who requested, approved, rejected, or cancelled a leave request.
_Avoid_: Employee directory, calendar
